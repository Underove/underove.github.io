Research Interests

Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

How can learning agents coordinate decisions across coupled manufacturing processes? Motivated by my thermal-process agent research, I want to study cooperation, credit assignment, and the balance between local objectives and plant-level constraints under changing operating conditions.

Reinforcement Learning for Manufacturing Control

I am interested in data-efficient policies that balance temperature accuracy, thermal uniformity, and heat input within operating limits. My focus is on offline learning, simulation-based evaluation, and constraint-aware policy improvement for thermal processes.

Industrial AI Agents

Building on my KETI agent-design work and NOVA, I want to connect LLM-based context interpretation and task planning with prediction and specialized control modules. I am interested in reliable tool use and feedback, with explicit safety checks and operator approval for operating-condition recommendations.
